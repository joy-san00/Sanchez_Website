"A Hand-Made Wallet"

An ill child was brought to a hospital. After her treatment the mother showed the doctor a hand-made wallet and said:

“There are things we can’t pay, I hope you take this wallet I made for you”

The doctor said:

“I don’t practice for sentimental reasons. Pay me 10000.”

The lady opened the wallet, took out P5000 and gave the doctor 1000. Then she replied:

“You are a very good Doctor but I wish you were a better person.”
